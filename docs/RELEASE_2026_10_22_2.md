# 2026.10.22.2 — Castlevania skeleton safety

Includes all repairs from 2026.10.22.1.

Castlevania skeleton palette imports could release shared original images that later enemy spawns still required. The result included missing skeletons and an unrelated large image in an animation. The two affected palette targets now keep their native images and animations. Their random recoloring is deliberately disabled; other actor palettes, scenery, backgrounds and floor options remain available. Saved routes, seeds and rewards are unchanged.

Validation: 408 automated tests including native coverage for untouched skeleton image banks; twelve isolated Linux/Wine scene reloads at four Castlevania positions with no image refusals or errors, visible skeletons and advancing frames. Three real Ninja Gaiden enemy kills produced the original 48 × 48 effect without custom player blood or death-sound activation; the isolated game audio recording contained no silent 100 ms windows after its first second.

Laboratory tests used god mode and controlled positioning. This is not an all-boss or all-seed playthrough. Mario's final enemy phase and every natural boss exit still require separate live confirmation. This is a local repair build, not a published release.
