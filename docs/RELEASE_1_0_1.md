# Boshy Randomizer 1.0.1

Your window stays the size you chose when you reset or move to another room. You can still change the resolution and aspect ratio in Options.

Install over your existing version to keep your saves and settings.

Original I Wanna Be The Boshy by **Solgryn / Grynsoft**. Randomizer mod by **THXel**.

Checked on Windows 11 and Linux/Wine. [Website](https://thxel.github.io/Boshy-Randomizer/) · [Verification](https://github.com/THXel/Boshy-Randomizer/blob/v1.0.1/docs/RELEASE_VERIFICATION.md)
