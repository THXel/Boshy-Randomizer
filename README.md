## Five ways to play

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Randomizer** | Shuffled levels, bosses and chests, ending with Solgryn. |
| **Target Select** | Find your target items and defeat your boss targets to reach Solgryn. |
| **Normal** | Play the original route with your favourite optional extras. |
| **Boss Rush** | One boss after another. You choose the lineup. |
| **Level Rush** | Take on your chosen levels, then face Solgryn. |

## What’s beyond the next exit?

- **New paths through Boshy:** Shuffle levels, bosses and secret routes. You choose what goes in.
- **A surprise in every chest:** An item or a new character? Open it and find out.
- **Same seed. Who’s faster?:** Share your seed with friends and race through the same route.
- **Room for three runs:** One for practice, one for racing, one for chaos. Pick up where you left off.
- **Keep track of your run:** Time, deaths and collected items in LiveTracker – ready for your stream, too.
- **Solgryn awaits:** Reach the final fight and see how your run went.

## Boshy, shuffled inside out

Familiar worlds get different backgrounds, colours and sprites. You decide how wild it gets.

Let your shots change as you play and shuffle the soundtrack. Choose the original soundtrack or the Undertale pack. Optional Kamilia 3 and WannaFest22 packs bring their own music and death screens.

## Play your way

## Choose your platform

**Windows (.exe) · Linux (.deb) — Coming soon**

Bring your own Boshy folder or ZIP. The original game is not included. [Download the original I Wanna Be The Boshy from Grynsoft](https://grynsoft.com/site/game/i-wanna-be-the-boshy)

The randomizer creates its own game copy. Your original game and saves stay separate.

## Good to know

<details>
<summary>Can I use it for a stream?</summary>

Yes. Open the built-in LiveTracker from the game menu and use it in your browser or as an OBS browser source.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What if a trigger behaves strangely?</summary>

Open the diagnostics in the game menu and include the mode, seed and a description of what happened in your GitHub report.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Who has tested the randomizer?</summary>

THXel is the only playtester so far. Final testing for the first release is underway.

</details>

<details>
<summary>When can I download it?</summary>

The first final release is being prepared. Windows Setup and Linux DEB will appear here once it is ready. No release date is confirmed.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I play online?</summary>

This is a single-player randomizer. The old Boshy online menu is disabled. Share seed codes to play comparable runs with friends.

</details>
