## Five ways to play

| Mode | |
| --- | --- |
| **Randomizer** | **The familiar game, remixed.** Shuffle level and boss destinations, and mix items and characters across both kinds of chest. Advanced settings let you choose the pools and counts. Recommended: seven levels and seven bosses, alternating where the selected pools allow, then Solgryn. |
| **Target Select** | **A checklist. An unpredictable journey.** Chase a random list of original items, with an adjustable target count and optional required bosses. Destinations change; chest rewards stay original. The route continues until your goals are complete, then opens the Solgryn finale. |
| **Normal** | **Your route. Your rules.** Keep the original progression and chest rewards. Choose a starting character and optionally enable character rotation or hacks. These extras initially stay off in Normal mode. |
| **Boss Rush** | **Skip straight to the fights.** Choose the boss pool and how many encounters you want. Seven is the default. After the chosen fights, face Solgryn as the finale. |
| **Level Rush** | **Make the worlds your challenge.** Choose the level pool and number of sections. Seven is the default. Finish the selected sections, then face Solgryn as the finale. |

## Make every run your own

### Routes beyond the usual path

Split worlds, minibosses and secret routes sit alongside beta fights such as Ridley, Questionmark, Bomberman and Old Sonic. Advanced lets you include the encounters you want. Transitions use game state instead of screen pixels.

### Items & characters, one reward pool

A chest can award an item or a character regardless of its original type. Optional character rotation draws from your available cast; newly collected characters join the pool. F3 selection is locked while rotation is active.

### A seed worth sharing

Compact share codes carry the seed and run settings. Copy, paste, validate, preview or generate a new code in the game menu. Use the same randomizer version when comparing runs.

### Three slots. Three adventures.

Each slot keeps its own run and character unlocks. Menu summaries show your location, difficulty, selected character and progress. Resume, create or delete saves from the native menu.

### A tracker beside the game

A separate window keeps the playfield clear: timer, deaths, location, collected items and characters, boss progress and copy-seed. It adapts to the mode, highlights Target Select goals, uses compact icons and announces new pickups.

### An ending you can enjoy

After Solgryn, a delayed, lightly transparent results screen brings together the seed and run statistics, with a copy button. The death animation and credits continue behind it.

![Native run settings and Advanced](docs/media/rediscover-menu-tour.gif)

### A familiar game with an unfamiliar look

New intro casts and scenery on launch. Optional Sprite Chaos reshuffles reviewed backgrounds with their animated parallax, decoration and platform materials. Configure the layers separately and dim backgrounds for clarity. Enemy and boss colour changes are beta; their shapes, attacks and collision geometry stay intact.

### New shots. New sound.

Randomize projectiles and their firing effects independently of your character: after a spot, after a death, after both, or at a random time within your chosen limit. Set that limit in seconds or minutes; the default is five minutes.

Music Shuffle mixes level and boss music together with a seed-based assignment. Optional locally installed Kamilia 3 and WannaFest22 soundpacks can also replace matching intro, menu, death and ending cues. Soundpack selection is remembered; shuffle tracks must be longer than 30 seconds. These extra soundpacks are not included in the downloads. Locally imported pack graphics add matching animated death screens and blood sprites: Kamilia fades red before its title, while WannaFest slams its title in over VHS noise. Original reset behavior stays unchanged.

## A little help. A little chaos.

Choose optional tweaks under Advanced → Hacks. Make a practice run, change the feel, or leave a surprise waiting.

- Suppress disruptive item pop-ups
- Disable the mouse ghost
- One-HP bosses, per vulnerable phase
- God Mode
- Autofire while holding shoot
- Mirror mode
- Change character after death
- Automatically skip boss intros
- Shorten chest animations
- Shuffle music
- Configure Sprite Chaos
- Spider surprise

> The spider surprise starts enabled for new randomizer runs: an unannounced attack every 5–30 active gameplay minutes, including boss rooms. Turn it off and apply the setting to remember that preference for future runs. Saved runs and imported seeds keep their own rules.

### Built for the way you play

- Mouse hover and keyboard navigation; Enter confirms selections.
- Menus adapt to the game window, with a separate text-size setting.
- Modern monitor-fitting sizes in the original Options menu; stretched fullscreen remains available.
- English, German, Russian, Spanish and Japanese; automatic system-language selection with English fallback. Update logs stay English.

## Good to know

<details>
<summary>Can I use it for a stream?</summary>

The built-in live tracker is a separate window that you can arrange beside the game or capture in OBS.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What if a trigger behaves strangely?</summary>

Ctrl+Alt+D opens diagnostics with live state, test actions and exportable recordings. Ctrl+Alt+M marks a problem and saves recent diagnostic history without opening a window. Reports can include a game-only screenshot.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is every combination tested?</summary>

369 automated source tests pass. Windows installation, update, uninstall, save preservation and resume were checked with Defender enabled. Linux package checks are recorded in the release notes. Every complete route and hack combination has not been played through.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What does Windows preview mean?</summary>

The installer is unsigned and has been tested in Windows 10 x64. Windows 11, non-ASCII account paths, physical 4K and mixed-DPI setups still need dedicated checks. Keep antivirus protection enabled.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I play online?</summary>

This is a single-player randomizer. The old Boshy online menu is disabled. Share seed codes to play comparable runs with friends.

</details>
