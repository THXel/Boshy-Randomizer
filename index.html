Meet Boshy Randomizer

Boshy Randomizer is nearing its first final release. So far, THXel is the only playtester. The download will be available here when the first final version is ready.

Clear game menus

Two-column categories, meaningful icons, contextual help and mouse or keyboard controls. Your seed is visible at a glance.

Save slots with useful details

Preview your location and collection. Choose newly collected characters with F3 when character selection is unlocked.

Your screen, your options

Classic 4:3 or stretched 16:9, F4 fullscreen and saved language and soundtrack preferences. Optional No Screen Shaking.

Consistent backgrounds and sound

Complete backgrounds with matching layers and effects. Separate music pools for levels and bosses; soundpack preferences stay outside the run seed.

Live tracking and the finale

LiveTracker follows the game language. Results appear over the credits, close with × and disappear on F2 restart.

Installation with clear feedback

Progress indicators, cancellation during preparation and optional launch on completion. Import your own original Boshy ZIP or folder.

For new Randomizer and Boss Rush runs, Bomberman, Questionmark and Ridley start disabled. You can enable them explicitly; existing saves and imported seeds keep their choices.

Test status and details

Gameplay has so far been tested only by THXel. Automated checks support development, but there has been no wider public playtest. Final checks are still in progress before release.

Explore the menus

New GIF tours assembled from current test screenshots: menus, appearance settings and save slots. These are animated screenshot sequences, not continuous gameplay recordings.

01 / PLAY

Five ways to play

What do you feel like playing?

  1. Surprise me

    Randomizer

    The familiar game, remixed.

    Shuffle level and boss destinations, and mix items and characters across both kinds of chest. Route settings let you choose the pools and counts. Recommended: seven levels and seven bosses, alternating where the selected pools allow, then Solgryn.

  2. Give me collection goals

    Target Select

    A checklist. An unpredictable journey.

    Chase a random list of original items, with an adjustable target count and optional required bosses. Destinations change; chest rewards stay original. The route continues until your goals are complete, then opens the Solgryn finale.

  3. Keep the original route

    Normal

    Your route. Your rules.

    Keep the original progression and chest rewards. Choose a starting character and optionally enable character rotation or hacks. These extras initially stay off in Normal mode.

  4. Just the bosses

    Boss Rush

    Skip straight to the fights.

    Choose the boss pool and how many encounters you want. Seven is the default. After the chosen fights, face Solgryn as the finale.

  5. Mostly platforming

    Level Rush

    Make the worlds your challenge.

    Choose the level pool and number of sections. Seven is the default. Finish the selected sections, then face Solgryn as the finale.

02 / REMIX

Make every run your own

↗

Routes beyond the usual path

Split worlds, minibosses and secret routes sit alongside beta fights such as Ridley, Questionmark, Bomberman and Old Sonic. Route lets you include the encounters you want. Transitions use game state instead of screen pixels.

✦

Items & characters, one reward pool

A chest can award an item or a character regardless of its original type. Optional character rotation draws from your available cast; newly collected characters join the pool. F3 selection is locked while rotation is active.

#

A seed worth sharing

Compact share codes carry the seed and run settings. Copy, paste, validate, preview or generate a new code in the game menu. Use the same randomizer version when comparing runs.

III

Three slots. Three adventures.

Each slot keeps its own run and character unlocks. Menu summaries show your location, difficulty, selected character and progress. Resume, create or delete saves from the native menu.

◷

A tracker beside the game

Open LiveTracker from the main menu in your browser. It follows the game language and displays time, deaths, location and collected items and characters. You can also use it in OBS.

★

An ending you can enjoy

After Solgryn, a delayed, lightly transparent results screen brings together the seed and run statistics, with a copy button. The death animation and credits continue behind it.

NEW LOOK · SAME BOSHY

A familiar game with an unfamiliar look

Backgrounds are shuffled as complete groups with their matching layers and effects. Decoration, platforms and enemy colours have separate options. Important gameplay effects stay protected.

New shots. New sound.

Randomize projectiles and their firing effects independently of your character: after a spot, after a death, after both, or at a random time within your chosen limit. Set that limit in seconds or minutes; the default is five minutes.

Music Shuffle assigns level tracks to levels and boss tracks to bosses, using separate pools. Optional locally imported Kamilia 3 and WannaFest22 packs also provide matching music and effects. Additional soundpacks are not included in the downloads.

How installation will work

For the first final release: you will need your own original Boshy ZIP or folder. The game itself is not included.

Windows

  1. Install

    Download and open Windows Setup. Follow the wizard to install Boshy Randomizer.

  2. Select the original game

    In the setup wizard, select your original Boshy ZIP or folder. The randomizer creates its own game copy.

  3. Play

    Choose one of the three save slots, select a mode and start your run.

Linux

  1. Install the DEB

    Open the DEB with your package installer and install it with its dependencies, including Wine and Lutris.

  2. Complete first-time setup

    Open Boshy Randomizer from the applications menu. Allow the first Wine setup to finish, then select your original Boshy folder or ZIP.

  3. Play

    Choose a save slot and mode. For later runs, open Boshy Randomizer again from your applications menu.

The randomizer creates a private game copy. Your original saves stay untouched; package updates and removal preserve randomizer save data.

06 / SUPPORT

Good to know

Can I use it for a stream?

The built-in live tracker is a separate window that you can arrange beside the game or capture in OBS.

What if a trigger behaves strangely?

Open the diagnostics in the game menu and include the mode, seed and a description of what happened in your GitHub report.

Who has tested the randomizer?

So far, only THXel has playtested it. The project is close to its first final release; no wider public testing has taken place.

When can I download it?

The first final release is being prepared. Windows Setup and Linux DEB will appear here once it is ready. No release date is confirmed.

Can I play online?

This is a single-player randomizer. The old Boshy online menu is disabled. Share seed codes to play comparable runs with friends.
